Practice guide & examples

This guitar trainer helps you practice scales, modes, triads, four-part chords, and arpeggios. Choose the elements you want to randomize, then use each result to practice immediate recall on the fretboard.

Start by selecting your practice keys and root-note spelling options. Enable only the reels named in an exercise, then click Roll again. The examples below show possible results, not predetermined rolls.

Scales & modes

Simple scales

  1. Enable Root note only.
  2. Click Roll again.
  3. Play the major scale starting on the indicated root note.

Modes from scale degrees

  1. Enable Root note and Scale degree.
  2. Click Roll again.
  3. Find the indicated degree of the selected parent scale and play the appropriate mode shape starting on that note.
Example: Root note C + scale degree 5 (C major parent scale) → play G Mixolydian.

Modes from a named root

  1. Enable Root note and Mode.
  2. Click Roll again.
  3. Play the indicated mode starting on the indicated root.
Example: Root note C + mode Phrygian → play C Phrygian.
Triads

Ascending & descending

  1. Enable Root note and Triad quality.
  2. Click Roll again.
  3. Play the indicated triad ascending and descending across the neck.
Example: Root note B♭ + quality Aug → play a B♭ augmented triad ascending and descending across the neck.

Triad inversions within a scale exercise

  1. Enable Root note, Triad quality, and Triad inversion.
  2. Click Roll again.
  3. Practice the major scale with the movable triad of the indicated quality and inversion, as described in the example.
Example: Root note F + quality Aug + inversion 2nd → play an F major scale with a movable F augmented triad in second inversion.
Arpeggios & four-part chords

Four-part chord arpeggios

  1. Enable Root note and Four-part chord shape.
  2. Click Roll again.
  3. Play the indicated chord as an arpeggio beginning on the root.
Example: Root note C + chord Min7♭5 → play C minor seven flat five across one octave:

1 — C · ♭3 — E♭ · ♭5 — G♭ · ♭7 — B♭ · 1 — C

Correction to the uploaded example: The document lists A♭ for the ♭7 of C. The correct ♭7 is B♭; A♭ is the ♭6. The example above reflects this correction.
